A

Anchor geometry

E00-E05

E00 baseline established the incumbent. E03 anchors 8/16/32/64 raised recall substantially but unmatched predictions increased greatly, so the result was HOLD. E04 larger anchors regressed.

B

RPN / proposals

E06-E09

E06 showed proposal recall improved when the RPN budget increased; E02 current-budget proposal recall@0.50 was 0.7565. The final detector still remained budget/score limited.

C

ROI / score behavior

E10

E10 found POSTPROCESSING_SATURATED and ROI_SCORE_LIMITED: localization was usable, but score discrimination was limited.

D

Learning rate and scheduler

E12-E14

E12 LR 0.0025 regressed. E13 LR 0.010 improved recall but produced excessive unmatched predictions. E14 scheduler was HOLD.

E

Partial backbone unfreeze

E15

E15 reached very high single-run recall 0.8927, but score inflation and 2,843 unmatched predictions made it HOLD.

F

Augmentation

E16-E17

Horizontal flip did not provide meaningful improvement. Brightness/contrast augmentation regressed the operating trade-off.

G

Input resolution

E19

Higher input resolution was promising, but not stable enough at the operating threshold to replace the incumbent.

H

Threshold/frontier analysis

E20

E20 compared calibrated frontier finalists: E19 @ 0.15 and E15 @ 0.175 were sent to multi-seed confirmation.

I

Multi-seed robustness

E21

Five-seed study concluded MULTISEED_LEADER. Single-run improvements were not reliable enough across seeds.
